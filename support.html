App Support

Welcome to the support page for Mad Fer It, an independently developed, unofficial fan trivia application.

About Mad Fer It

Mad Fer It is an unofficial trivia game featuring over 1,000 questions covering songs, albums, B-sides, gigs and the history of Oasis.

Having a Problem?

If you experience a problem while using Mad Fer It, please make a note of what happened, which part of the app you were using and, where possible, the version of iOS installed on your device.

Support information and known issues may be published on this page as updates become available.
