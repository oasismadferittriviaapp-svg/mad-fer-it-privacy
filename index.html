Mad Fer It

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026

Mad Fer It is an independently developed, unofficial fan trivia application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mad Fer It app.

Information We Collect

Mad Fer It does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number or postal address.

Mad Fer It does not intentionally collect personal information from users for analytics, advertising or profiling.

Quiz Data

Information required for the operation of the trivia game, such as quiz progress and previously encountered questions, may be stored locally on your device.

This information is used to operate the game and improve the variety of questions presented during play. This locally stored quiz information is not associated with an account or used to identify you.

Removing the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to the behaviour of your device and operating system.

Amazon Affiliate Links

Mad Fer It may recommend relevant products based on a player's quiz result.

Some product links are Amazon affiliate links. If you choose to follow one of these links, you will be taken to Amazon, where Amazon's own terms, privacy policy and data-handling practices apply.

Mad Fer It may receive a commission from qualifying purchases made through eligible affiliate links, at no additional cost to you.

Mad Fer It does not require you to make a purchase in order to use the trivia game.

Third-Party Services and External Links

Mad Fer It may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Once you follow an external link, your interaction with that third party is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. Mad Fer It is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Advertising and Tracking

Mad Fer It does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or analytics services and does not intentionally track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Retention and Deletion

Mad Fer It does not currently maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database containing users' personal information.

Game information stored locally on your device can be removed by deleting the app, subject to your device's operating-system behaviour.

Because Mad Fer It does not currently maintain user accounts or a developer-operated store of personal information, there is no corresponding server-side user account or personal-data record to delete.

Children's Privacy

Mad Fer It is a general-audience trivia application and is not specifically directed at children.

Mad Fer It does not knowingly request personal information from children through an account or registration system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Mad Fer It's features or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy URL.
